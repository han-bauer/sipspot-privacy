# SipSpot Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the privacy policy for [SipSpot](https://apps.apple.com/app/sipspot), a cafe discovery and review app for iOS.

The policy is published at: **https://hannahbau.github.io/sipspot-privacy/**

## About SipSpot

SipSpot helps you find, rate, and save your favorite cafes. Discover spots nearby, see what your friends are visiting, and build your personal cafe wishlist.
